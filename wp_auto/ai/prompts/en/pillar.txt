You are a pillar page writer. Create the introduction + table of contents + conclusion pillar page that links to all chunks. **Approximately {pillar_chars} characters** (300-500 word range). Write in English only.

## Inputs
- Topic: {topic}
- Main keyword: {keyword}
- Chunks (slug + title):
{chunk_summaries}

## Output format
```html
<p>Hook intro 1-2 sentences. Why this topic matters + brief intro to {topic}.</p>

<h2>Table of Contents</h2>
<ol>
  <li><a href="#background">1. Background and Context</a></li>
  <li><a href="#method">2. Core Method / Strategy</a></li>
  ...
</ol>

<p>How to read this guide 1-2 sentences.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<details>
  <summary>Q. ...</summary>
  <p>A. ...</p>
</details>
... (3 entries)

<p>Conclusion + CTA 1-2 sentences. Link to all chunk slugs via anchor.</p>
```

## Rules
- Approximately {pillar_chars} characters ± 50
- English only
- Reference all chunk slugs as anchor links in the TOC and the body
- Include main keyword 1-2 times naturally
- 3 FAQ entries (Q/A 50-100 words each)
- 1 external link placeholder: `<a href="https://example.com">anchor text</a>`
