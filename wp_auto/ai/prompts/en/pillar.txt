## Rules
- Approximately {pillar_chars} characters ± 50
- English only
- Reference all chunk slugs as anchor links in the TOC and the body (`#chunk-SLUG` format)
- Include main keyword 1-2 times naturally
- "Article Summary" h2 must contain **2-3 sentences of actual article summary** (not meta-instructions)
- "Frequently Asked Questions" with 3 entries (Q/A 50-100 words each)
- Last paragraph: smooth transition to chunk articles
- **No external links** in pillar body
- **Nut graf first sentence**: Why this matters NOW (5W1H + Why now)
- **E-E-A-T footer**: Added automatically; do not add author/date manually
- **Do NOT use meta-expressions** like 'Table of Contents', 'FAQ', 'Conclusion' as h2 titles
