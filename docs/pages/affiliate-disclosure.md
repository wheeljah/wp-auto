## Affiliate Disclosure

**Last updated**: August 5, 2026

Dopaminews is a **multi-category news aggregator**, not a review site or affiliate-driven product blog. In accordance with the **U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guidelines** (16 CFR Part 255) and the operating agreements of affiliate networks we participate in, this page explains how we earn revenue and how that affects our news coverage.

---

### 1. The short version

> **Dopaminews is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, Awin, and CJ Affiliate — advertising programs designed to provide a means for sites to earn fees by linking to amazon.com, awin.com advertisers, and CJ.com advertisers.**
>
> **When you click an affiliate link in a story, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you.**
>
> **News coverage is independent of affiliate relationships. We cover news whether or not an affiliate link exists for the product or topic being discussed.**

---

### 2. How affiliate links appear in a news aggregator

Unlike review sites, **Dopaminews does not exist to sell products**. We exist to summarize and link to news. Affiliate links appear in **two narrow contexts**:

#### 2.1 "Further reading" links (book / documentary / podcast)

When we cover a story that relates to a book, documentary, or podcast, we may link to it on Amazon (or wherever it's sold). The link is **descriptive** (e.g., "Buy on Amazon" or "Read *Thinking, Fast and Slow*"), not a call-to-action to buy.

#### 2.2 "Tools mentioned in this story" links

When a news story mentions a specific product, tool, or service (e.g., a research tool used in a study, a platform mentioned in a funding round), we may link to it via an affiliate link. These links are clearly marked `[affiliate]` at the end of the section.

> **We never write or pick a story to drive affiliate sales.** Editorial selection is based on newsworthiness, not on whether we have an affiliate link.

---

### 3. FTC compliance (16 CFR Part 255)

The FTC requires that affiliate relationships be **clearly and conspicuously disclosed**. To meet this standard, we:

1. **Disclose at the top of every story** that contains affiliate links (banner: "This story contains affiliate links")
2. **Mark each affiliate link** with `[affiliate]` or `rel="sponsored"` (not buried in the text)
3. **Use plain language** — not "disclosure" footnotes hidden behind links
4. **Tag every affiliate link** with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` so search engines and screen readers understand the commercial nature
5. **Never use the phrase "click here"** for affiliate anchor text — we use descriptive text (per Amazon Operating Agreement)

> **Source**: [FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/marketing-advertising/endorsements-influencers-reviews) — 16 CFR Part 255.5 requires disclosure whenever there is a "material connection" between an endorser and marketer.

### 4. Amazon Associates Program-specific disclosure

> **"We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for us to earn fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites."**

- **24-hour cookie**: When you click an Amazon affiliate link, Amazon places a 24-hour cookie in your browser. If you buy *anything* on Amazon within 24 hours (even a different product), we may earn a small commission (typically 1-4% of the purchase price, depending on category).
- **Associates Program Operating Agreement**: We follow [Amazon's operating requirements](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement) — including no affiliate links in offline PDFs, no cloaking, and no incentivized clicks.
- **Price display**: Prices shown via Amazon affiliate links are approximate and may change. Always check Amazon.com for the current price.

> **Note for news context**: Amazon Associates permits affiliate links in editorial content (including news) as long as the article is genuinely editorial and not a "review" disguised as news. We comply by ensuring each Amazon link is in a "where to find" context, not a "you should buy" context.

### 5. Awin disclosure

> **"Dopaminews is a participant in Awin, a global affiliate network. When you click an Awin-tracked link and make a purchase from one of Awin's 25,000+ advertiser partners, we may earn a commission."**

- **30-day cookie** (advertiser-dependent; some are 7, 14, or 30 days)
- **Advertisers we may link to via Awin**: include but are not limited to Shopify merchants, ASOS, Nike, and other brands relevant to the stories we cover
- **Awin Privacy**: Awin sets its own tracking cookies — see [Awin's privacy policy](https://www.awin.com/gb/privacy-policy)

### 6. CJ Affiliate disclosure

> **"Dopaminews is a participant in CJ Affiliate (formerly Commission Junction), a global affiliate marketing network. When you click a CJ-tracked link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission."**

- **Cookie duration**: Advertiser-specific (typically 30-60 days)
- **Advertisers we may link to via CJ**: include US/global brands relevant to the stories we cover (productivity tools, books, tech, lifestyle)
- **CJ Privacy**: CJ sets its own tracking cookies — see [CJ's privacy policy](https://www.cj.com/legal/privacy-policy)

### 7. What we **never** do

To maintain news integrity and FTC compliance, we explicitly **do not**:

- ❌ **Cover a story to drive affiliate sales** — editorial selection comes first
- ❌ **Bury affiliate disclosures** — every story with affiliate links has a visible banner
- ❌ **Promote a product as "news"** without a primary source (press release ≠ news)
- ❌ **Use fake urgency** ("Only 3 left!" / "Sale ends in 1 hour!") unless the merchant itself publishes that
- ❌ **Cloak affiliate links** — all affiliate links are visible (you can see `amazon.com`, `awin.com`, etc. in the URL bar)
- ❌ **Use "click here" anchor text** for affiliate links (per Amazon Operating Agreement)
- ❌ **Suppress negative news** about an advertiser (we cover research that contradicts marketed claims)
- ❌ **Edit stories retroactively** to remove criticism (corrections only, with date stamp)

### 8. Editorial policy (news aggregator context)

Our news site operates under a clear hierarchy:

```
PRIMARY SOURCE EXISTS
   ↓ (verified)
NEWSWORTHINESS CHECK (timely, relevant, credible)
   ↓ (filtered)
EDITORIAL DECISION (what to cover)
   ↓ (paraphrased + cited)
STORY PUBLISHED
   ↓ (optional, context-only)
AFFILIATE LINK (only if natural fit)
```

Affiliate links are added **after** editorial decision, never before. If a product is newsworthy but we don't have an affiliate relationship, we still cover it — and link to the product's official site (no commission).

---

### 9. How we choose what to cover

Our editorial selection process:

1. **Is there a new primary source?** (peer-reviewed paper, official press release, major news outlet, regulatory filing)
2. **Is it relevant to one of our 6 categories?** (Tech & AI / Business & Finance / Science & Health / World & Politics / Climate & Environment / Culture & Media)
3. **Is it timely?** (within the last 24-48 hours for breaking news; within the week for analysis)
4. **Is the source credible?** (Tier 1 outlets, established researchers, official company statements, regulatory bodies)
5. **Will it interest our readers?** (would the average news reader want to know this?)

Affiliate availability is **never** a factor in editorial selection. We cover what matters, not what's profitable.

### 10. Affiliate link tagging (technical)

Every affiliate link in our HTML output is automatically tagged:

```html
<a href="https://amazon.com/..." rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">
  Thinking, Fast and Slow on Amazon [affiliate]
</a>
```

- `rel="sponsored"` — tells search engines this is a paid/affiliate link
- `rel="nofollow"` — prevents passing PageRank to the advertiser
- `rel="noopener"` — security best practice for `target="_blank"`
- `[affiliate]` suffix — visible disclosure adjacent to the link
- Descriptive anchor text — instead of "click here"

This is implemented via [wp-auto](https://github.com/wheeljah/wp-auto)'s `affiliate_linker.py` module, which handles all 3 networks (Amazon, Awin, CJ) consistently.

### 11. Income disclosure (transparency)

Dopaminews is a 1-person operation. As of the last update of this page, **affiliate commissions cover hosting + domain costs and provide a small side income** — not a full-time salary.

We disclose this so you understand:

- We are not "professional affiliate marketers" optimizing for maximum commission
- We will turn down affiliate programs that don't fit our editorial standards
- The site's existence does **not** depend on any single affiliate relationship
