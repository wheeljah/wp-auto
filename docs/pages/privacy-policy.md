## Privacy Policy

**Last updated**: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dopaminews ("we", "us", "our", "https://dopaminews.com") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our news aggregator.

By using the site, you agree to the practices described below.

---

### 1. Information we collect

We collect **only the minimum** information needed to operate a news aggregator. Specifically:

#### 1.1 Automatically collected (server logs)

- IP address (anonymized after 30 days via Cloudflare)
- Browser type / version
- Referring page (the news source you came from, if any)
- Pages visited + timestamps (which stories you read)
- Cloudflare edge response codes

> These are standard server logs retained **30 days** for security + abuse detection. We use **aggregated, anonymized** traffic data to understand which categories are most read — we do not profile individual readers.

#### 1.2 Cookies we set

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__test` | First-party (Cloudflare) | Bot detection (JS challenge verification) | Session |
| `wordpress_logged_in_*` | First-party (WordPress) | Keep you logged in (only set if you log in) | 14 days |
| `wp-settings-*` | First-party (WordPress) | Remember UI preferences (logged-in users) | 1 year |
| Analytics cookies | **Not used** | — | — |
| Newsletter cookies | **Not used** (no newsletter yet) | — | — |

> We do **not** use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any cross-site advertising cookies.

#### 1.3 Information you provide directly

- **Email address** (only if you submit the contact form OR subscribe to a future newsletter) — used to reply/deliver the newsletter, not shared with third parties
- **Comments** (if you leave one on a story) — publicly displayed with the name/email you enter

#### 1.4 Information from third-party news sources

When you click a primary source link in a story, you leave our site and the destination site (NYT, Reuters, arXiv, etc.) may set its own cookies. **We do not control those practices** — see [Third-party services](#3-third-party-services-we-use).

---

### 2. How we use your information

We use the limited information we collect for:

1. **Operating the site** — serving pages, preventing abuse, debugging errors
2. **Responding to you** — replying to contact-form submissions
3. **Newsletter delivery** (when launched) — sending the weekly roundup to subscribers
4. **Compliance** — meeting Amazon/Awin/CJ Affiliate program requirements
5. **News analytics** — understanding which categories / stories are most read (aggregated only)

We do **not**:

- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Use your data for behavioral advertising profiling
- Share your email with any third party (except as needed to deliver the newsletter)
- Track you across other websites
- Add you to a marketing list because you submitted the contact form

### 4. Comments policy

News sites get more comments than personal blogs. Our approach:

- **All comments are moderated manually** before publishing (we have a 1-person team)
- We do **not** display your email publicly (only name + comment)
- We retain comments **indefinitely** unless you request deletion (older comments are part of the story's record)
- We may **edit comments for length or clarity** with a `[edited by moderator]` note
- We **delete** comments that are spam, harassment, off-topic, or contain unverified claims about ongoing stories

To request deletion of a comment you posted, use the [Contact form](/contact).

> **Note for news**: Comments on breaking news stories are held for moderation more strictly (we wait for facts to settle before publishing opinions). Comments on evergreen / analysis pieces are moderated within 24 hours.

### 5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data (email, comment, newsletter subscription) — contact us via the [Contact form](/contact)
- **Object** to processing
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority

> Since we don't maintain a marketing email list or user database (other than newsletter subscribers when launched), most of these rights apply to **comment data** and **contact form submissions** only.

### 6. Children's privacy

Dopaminews is not directed at children under 13 (COPPA) or 16 (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted data via the contact form or comments, please contact us for deletion.

### 9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards:

- **HTTPS everywhere** (Cloudflare Universal SSL + Full mode)
- **Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode** (free tier, blocks common attacks)
- **Wordfence Security** plugin (WAF + malware scan)
- **No password storage of reader data** (only site admin accounts)
- **Comment moderation** (manual, prevents spam + injection)

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please contact us responsibly.
