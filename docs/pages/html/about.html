About Dopaminews

Dopaminews is a 1-person news site that curates the day's most addictive, essential stories across tech, business, science, world, climate, and culture — so you can stay informed in 10 minutes a day without doomscrolling.

The name says it all: "dopamine" for the satisfying, can't-stop-reading feel, "news" for what we cover. We aim to be the most essential news source on your morning coffee break.

What we cover

We aggregate and summarize stories from primary sources across 6 categories:

Category What we cover Cadence
Tech & AI New tools, models, funding, regulation 2-4 stories/day
Business & Finance M&A, IPOs, earnings, market moves, central bank policy 1-3 stories/day
Science & Health Peer-reviewed papers, FDA actions, public health 1-2 stories/day
World & Politics Major international events, policy, elections 1-2 stories/day
Climate & Environment Climate science, energy transition, extreme weather 1 story/day
Culture & Media Streaming, books, gaming, creator economy 1-2 stories/day

We do not aggregate: sports scores, celebrity gossip, sponsored content, or anything without a verifiable primary source.

How we work (1-person, AI-assisted)

  • 1 curator (me) — picks the top 5-10 stories each morning from primary sources (Reuters, AP, NYT, TechCrunch, Nature, arXiv, Hacker News, etc.)
  • AI-assisted summarization — drafts are paraphrased + cited via wp-auto (Trafilatura for URL extraction + Ollama for outline)
  • Manual editorial pass — every story is rewritten for clarity, fact-checked against the primary source, and tagged with category
  • Published within hours of the original story

We never copy-paste from news outlets. Every story is paraphrased, cited, and attributed. Press releases are flagged as such.

What we never do

  • ❌ Republish press releases as news — we always label them "Press release" and add editorial context
  • ❌ Aggregate without primary source — every story links to its origin
  • ❌ Original reporting — we are a curator, not a reporter (we don't have sources; we summarize public ones)
  • ❌ Paywall circumvention — if a story is behind a paywall, we link to the headline + summarize the public preview only
  • ❌ Anonymous tips — we don't publish rumors or unverified claims

Editorial independence

Dopaminews is reader-supported via affiliate links and (eventually) newsletter sponsorships. Editorial decisions are made before any affiliate consideration. See our Affiliate Disclosure for the full policy.

We will:

  • ✅ Cover a story from any outlet, regardless of affiliate relationship
  • ✅ Post negative news about a brand that sponsors the newsletter
  • ✅ Correct errors quickly (visible correction note, dated)

We won't:

  • ❌ Accept payment to cover a story
  • ❌ Skip a story because we have no affiliate link
  • ❌ Edit old stories to remove criticism
