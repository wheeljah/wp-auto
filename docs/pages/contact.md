### What to contact us about

| Topic | Best for | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| **Story tip** | A primary source we should cover (paper, press release, official statement) | 1-2 days |
| **Source correction** | A story we got wrong, or a better primary source | 1-2 days |
| **Outreach / PR** | Press release, embargoed story, executive availability | 2-5 days |
| **Newsletter / sponsorship** | Brand partnership for the weekly roundup | 7-14 days |
| **Guest curator / pitch** | A category expert who wants to contribute | 7-14 days |
| **Tech issue / bug** | Site problem, broken link, accessibility | 1-2 days |

> **Important**: We are a **news aggregator**, not a news reporter. We do not:
> - Run original investigations
> - Publish anonymous tips (without a verifiable primary source)
> - Accept paid story placements
> - Republish press releases without editorial context

### How to send a story tip

The fastest way to get a story covered:

1. **Subject line**: `[TIP] {category} — {one-line summary}` (e.g., `[TIP] Tech — OpenAI announces GPT-6`)
2. **Body**:
   - **Primary source URL** (paper, press release, official statement — must be public)
   - **One-line summary** in your own words
   - **Why it matters** (1 sentence — what's the dopamine-worthy hook)
3. **Attach** PDF or screenshot only if the primary source is paywalled (we'll link to the public preview)

> We do not open attachments from unknown senders. If your tip is high-value but the source is paywalled, include the abstract + 1-2 key quotes in the body.

### Press / PR workflow

For PR professionals:

- **Email**: `press [at] dopaminews [dot] com`
- **Lead time**: 3-5 business days for embargoed stories
- **What we need**:
  - **Primary source URL** (or the press release itself, if public)
  - **Embargo date** (we honor it strictly)
  - **One-line pitch** (we treat every pitch on news merit, not placement)
- **What we don't accept**:
  - "We'll send you the press kit" without a story angle
  - "Sponsored content" pitches (we don't run sponsored news)
  - "Let's schedule a call" without an email summary first

> Per our [editorial policy](/about#editorial-independence), coverage is never guaranteed. We judge every story on its news merit + primary source quality.

### Corrections

If you spot an error in a story:

1. Use the [Contact form](/contact) with subject **"Correction request"**
2. Include the **URL** of the story
3. Cite a **primary source** showing the correct information
4. We'll respond within 48 hours with either a correction or a defense (with sources)

> Corrections are posted visibly at the top of the story with a date stamp. Major corrections are also announced in the next weekly roundup.
